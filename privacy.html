Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Vendy respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you play our game.

What we store

Game data such as your scores, puzzle progress, streaks, and preferences (like dark mode) may be stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

Analytics

We may use anonymous analytics tools to understand how people use Vendy. These tools may collect information such as device type, browser, general location, and session interactions. No personally identifiable information is collected.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, share, or transfer any personal data to third parties. Ever.

Children

Vendy is a word game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
